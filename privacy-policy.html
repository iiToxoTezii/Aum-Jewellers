Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 09, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Aum Jewellers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our store or use our website. Your trust is our most valued asset, and we are committed to safeguarding your privacy with the same meticulous care we apply to our jewellery.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, and transaction history. This occurs when you interact with our services, book exclusive bridal consultations, or visit our physical boutique.

3. Device Access & App Permissions

To provide a seamless mobile application experience, we may request permission to access your device's camera or photo gallery (to allow you to upload a profile avatar), and push notifications (to send you timely updates regarding your SIP portfolio and orders). You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect enables us to provide, maintain, and elevate our services. It helps us securely process your transactions, notify you of bespoke collections or important updates, and deliver the exceptional customer support that defines the Aum Jewellers experience.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application utilizes trusted third-party services, such as Google Firebase, for secure user authentication and database storage. These services collect and process data in accordance with their own strict privacy policies and security standards.

6. Data Security

We value the trust you place in us when sharing your personal details. To honor that trust, we employ industry-leading security protocols and strict confidentiality agreements to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You hold the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data. To initiate a data deletion request, please contact us directly via email or phone.

8. Policy Updates

As we evolve, so may our practices. We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operations or legal obligations. We will gracefully notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or require further clarification regarding our Privacy Policy or data deletion, please do not hesitate to contact us at the Aum Jewellers boutique or via our official contact channels.
